# Product Modules

If you're building a modern school management platform (Website + Android/iOS app), think of it as serving 5 groups of users:

- School Admin
- Principal/Management
- Teachers
- Students
- Parents

Here are feature ideas categorized by module.

- Role-based login
- Student login

## MVP (Version 1)

If you're starting from scratch, build only:

- Authentication & roles
- Student management
- Attendance
- Fees
- Homework
- Timetable
- Exams & report cards
- Notifications
- Parent app

This covers roughly 80% of what most schools need and can be expanded later into a full ERP.
